# Demo script · up to 2 minutes
**0:00–0:15 — A student’s decision.** “Imagine a text arrives saying you’ve won a prize. Before you tap anything, what can the wording tell you? ScamShield checks English SMS locally.”

**0:15–0:50 — Show a prediction.** Open index.html, click Try an example, then Check message. “The result is likely spam. Prize, won and free pushed the model toward that decision. The highlights show separate safety cues, including urgency and a link. Do not click suspicious links. For an account issue, open your bank’s official app independently.”

**0:50–1:15 — Show a mistake.** Expand the evaluation, copy its first public error into the checker and click Check message. “The dataset calls this ham. Someone is waiting for a call when the recipient is free. Look at the words the model picked up. A familiar word can mean something quite different in context.”

**1:15–1:45 — Compare.** “We removed repeated messages and held out one fifth, keeping a similar share of spam. Always guessing ham gets almost 88 percent right, yet misses all 128 spam messages. Our model caught 120 and wrongly flagged 11 ordinary texts.”

**1:45–2:00 — Close.** “Next I would test newer bank impersonation messages. Ham does not guarantee safety. This project is a prototype and is not financial advice.” Click Clear.
